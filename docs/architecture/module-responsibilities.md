# Module Responsibilities – TrueStock AI

This document defines the clear responsibilities of each module in the TrueStock AI system.

---

## 01. Product Management Module

**Responsible for** product information and catalog management.

- Create, update, and delete products
- Product categories and units
- SKU and barcode management
- Cost and selling prices
- CSV / Excel import and export

---

## 02. Inventory Management Module

**Responsible for** stock quantities and movements.

- Stock in and stock out
- Stock adjustments
- Warehouse and shop locations
- Stock transfers
- Stock movement history
- Stock-take sessions

---

## 03. Supplier & Purchasing Module

**Responsible for** suppliers and purchasing operations.

- Supplier registration
- Supplier lead-time management
- Purchase order creation
- Partial goods receiving
- Purchase history

---

## 04. Sales Management Module

**Responsible for** capturing sales information.

- Record sales
- Import sales data
- Sales history
- Product-level sales analysis
- Automatically reduce stock after sales

---

## 05. Business Intelligence Module

**Responsible for** analyzing business performance.

- Stock valuation
- Best-selling products
- Slow-moving products
- ABC analysis
- Days of stock cover
- Downloadable reports

---

## 06. AI Forecasting Module

**Responsible for** intelligent demand and reorder recommendations.

- Sales velocity calculations
- Moving averages
- Demand forecasting
- Reorder quantity recommendations
- Forecast accuracy tracking

---

## 07. Alerts & Notifications Module

**Responsible for** notifying users about important events.

- Low-stock alerts
- Out-of-stock alerts
- Near-expiry alerts
- Dead-stock alerts
- In-app and email notifications

---

## 08. Authentication & Tenant Management Module

**Responsible for** accounts, access, and business separation.

- Business registration
- User login and authentication
- Owner, manager, and staff roles
- Role-based permissions
- Multi-tenant data isolation
- Audit logs
